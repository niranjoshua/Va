Accuracy

Measured, not promised.

Every model is scored on held-out history against persistence, the forecast that assumes tomorrow looks like today, and, where one is published, against a grid operator's own forecast. If a model can't beat persistence, it doesn't ship.

453 held-out days of Belgian national load.1 Using calendar and past load only, no weather, Vaticore matched the day-ahead forecast Elia publishes, and its range was the better calibrated: 80.4% of outcomes inside the 80% band, against Elia's 74.9%. On solar, where tomorrow depends on the weather, Elia's weather-driven forecast is still far ahead. Weather is our next study.

What it's worth

Fewer blackouts, for a little more diesel in the right hours.

We replayed a full year the model had never seen. Every morning, each forecast planned the generator for the day ahead. Then the day ran on what actually happened, and we counted litres, outages and cost.

How it works

From meter data to a decision, every hour.

01

Connect

Send a CSV or stream readings over the API. Any resolution, any timezone, gaps expected. Everything is validated on the way in.

02

Forecast

A quantile model learns each site's demand and solar from calendar, recent history and weather, and sharpens as more data arrives.

03

Plan

The forecast becomes a reserve and backup recommendation with its range, refreshed as each new reading lands.
